## No service

Sim Bridge is software, not a service. The developer operates no relay, server or account for
you. You install the apps and run your own relay, and you are responsible for that relay: its
hosting, its owner key, and keeping it secure.

## Use it lawfully

Use Sim Bridge only with phones, SIM cards and messages you are allowed to access. Do not use it
to read other people's messages without their permission, to impersonate anyone, or for any
unlawful purpose.

## Carrier charges and reliability

Messages and calls go through your Android phone and your mobile carrier; your plan's charges and
rules apply. Delivery can fail when the phone is off, offline, restricted by the system, or out of
coverage. **Sim Bridge is not an emergency service.** Use your phone directly in an emergency.
