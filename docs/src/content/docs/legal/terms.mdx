## Authorized devices and communications

Use Sim Bridge only with devices, SIMs, messages, networks, and relays you are authorized to access. Obtain appropriate permission before linking another person’s device or exposing their communications to a bridge. Do not use the app for unauthorized interception, impersonation, abuse, or unlawful communications.

Anyone with bridge recovery material can gain shared bridge-key authority when enrollment is permitted. Keep that material private, select trusted devices and relays, and revoke unwanted enrollments. Removing a device does not automatically rotate a previously shared key or erase another device’s copies.

## Carrier charges and availability

SMS and calls are performed by the selected Android gateway and its carrier. Your carrier plan, roaming rules, charges, and legal requirements apply. The software does not include a mobile service plan, carry cellular audio to desktop/iPhone clients, or guarantee delivery, caller identity, or uninterrupted background operation.

Sim Bridge is not an emergency calling or safety-critical service. Network outages, revoked permissions, OS restrictions, sleeping devices, carrier failures, and unavailable gateways can prevent actions or alerts. Use the phone’s native carrier/emergency facilities where appropriate.
