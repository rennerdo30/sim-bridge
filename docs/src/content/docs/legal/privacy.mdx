Last updated: 3 November 2026. This policy applies to the Sim Bridge apps for Android, iOS,
iPadOS, macOS, Windows and Linux, and to this website.

## In short

- **We collect nothing.** The developer of Sim Bridge operates no server, relay, account
  system, analytics or advertising, and receives none of your messages, phone numbers,
  contacts, verification codes or usage data.
- **Your data stays with you.** Messages are stored on your own devices and pass only through
  a relay **you** run, for example in your own Cloudflare account or on your own computer.
- **End-to-end encrypted.** Everything that leaves a device is encrypted with AES-256-GCM
  under a key that only your paired devices hold. Your relay cannot read it.

## What the app processes on your devices

To work, Sim Bridge uses data on the devices you pair:

- **Android gateway:** incoming SMS, the SIM cards' names and numbers, and the state of phone
  calls, when you grant the SMS and phone permissions. It sends SMS and places calls only when
  you, or one of your paired devices, ask it to.
- **All devices:** the bridge's encryption key and each device's credentials, kept in the
  operating system's secure storage (such as the Keychain or the Android Keystore); the relay address, device names and settings; and the messages you have
  received, kept so you can read them.

This data never leaves your devices except encrypted, through your relay, to your other paired
devices.

## Your relay

The relay is software you deploy yourself; the Sim Bridge developer has no access to it. It
forwards only encrypted data. It keeps everything in memory, deletes encrypted messages and
commands after at most five minutes, and writes no database or log files.

To route data, your relay necessarily sees unencrypted metadata: device names and identifiers,
which devices belong to a bridge, the size and timing of messages, and the IP addresses of your
devices. Choose device names that reveal nothing sensitive. Whoever hosts your relay (for
example Cloudflare, or the provider of your server) processes this connection data under their
own terms and on your behalf.

## Other services involved

- **Your mobile carrier** delivers the SMS and calls your gateway sends, as for any SMS or call.
- **Apple Push Notification service**, only if you configure push on your relay: it delivers an
  encrypted notification to your iPhone. Apple sees the device token and delivery metadata, not
  the message.
- **GitHub**, when a desktop app checks for and downloads updates (at most once a day, and only
  if you keep automatic updates on): GitHub receives your IP address and the request, as for any
  download. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which see the visits.
- **App stores** (App Store, Google Play) process data about your download under their own
  policies.

The app contains no advertising, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Notifications and clipboard

Notifications can show message previews on your lock screen, depending on your system settings.
When you copy a verification or recovery code, it is placed on the clipboard; Sim Bridge marks it
as sensitive where the system supports that and clears it again after three minutes. Automatic
copying of codes is off by default on computers.

## Deleting your data

Everything Sim Bridge stores is on your devices and your relay. **Leave bridge** removes a
device's pairing and credentials; uninstalling the app removes its data from that device.
Restarting or deleting your relay removes everything it held. Messages already received by
another paired device remain there until you remove them on that device.

## Children

Sim Bridge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Your rights and contact

Because the developer collects no personal data, there is nothing we can show, correct or delete
on our side. If you email us, we use your email only to answer you and delete it when the request
is done. For questions or requests under data-protection laws such as the GDPR, contact
**[support@renner.dev](mailto:support@renner.dev)**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint
with a data-protection supervisory authority.

We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes; the date above shows the latest
version.
